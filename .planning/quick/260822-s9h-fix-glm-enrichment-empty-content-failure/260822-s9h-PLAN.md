<objective>
Fix GLM-4.7-Flash enrichment failing with empty content (finish_reason='length'). GLM is a reasoning model: under the hardcoded max_tokens=512 it spends its whole budget "thinking" and emits no content, so enrich_item_openai returns None and every candidate becomes a 'fetch_failed' tombstone.

Two complementary fixes: (1) raise/parameterize max_tokens as a safety net, and (2) disable GLM's thinking mode (10x cheaper, serves the bounded-LLM-spend constraint). Both are config-driven — no literals in application code.

Purpose: Restore working enrichment for the hosted GLM provider while honoring CLAUDE.md's "config, not code constant" discipline.
Output: Two new Tunables fields + env overrides, a parameterized OpenAI-compatible call with conditional extra_body, and the wiring that threads them through the enrichment loop.
</objective>
